03. CURRENTLY BUILDING

Automa

Programmable financial infrastructure for autonomous AI agents.

Automa explores a future where AI agents can manage money and execute financial transactions within clearly defined permissions.

The vision is to give agents secure, policy-controlled wallets so they can receive, hold, and spend digital assets while respecting user-defined budgets, spending limits, and transaction rules.

Focus areas: AI agents · Programmable wallets · Payment infrastructure · On-chain automation

My role: Founder, Founding Engineer & Growth Lead

The long-term vision is to make financial interactions between autonomous agents and digital services programmable, secure, and accessible to developers.

Kairo

Exploring liquidity infrastructure for NFT markets.

Kairo is an experimental NFT liquidity protocol concept focused on addressing the limitations of illiquid NFT assets.

The idea is to explore how NFT markets can become more flexible through liquidity mechanisms that connect NFTs, tokens, and decentralized financial infrastructure.

Focus areas: DeFi · NFT liquidity · Automated market makers · Monad ecosystem

My role: Product exploration & development

Nundina Finance

Private Credit. Reimagined.

At Nundina Finance, I work on growth initiatives for a Web3 financial product focused on the private credit secondary market.

My work involves building brand awareness, creating content, supporting community initiatives, and developing social strategies that help communicate the product's vision to the wider Web3 ecosystem.

Focus areas: Brand growth · Content strategy · Community building · Social media · Web3 marketing

My role: Growth team

04. SELECTED PROJECTS

Automa — Agentic Payments

Building toward a financial layer that enables AI agents to transact independently within programmable spending policies.

Kairo — NFT Liquidity Protocol

Exploring mechanisms that could help make NFT assets more liquid and useful within decentralized financial markets.
